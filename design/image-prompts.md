# flaner_cosmetics: image generation prompts

Four backgrounds and compositions for the Instagram shop. Paste each scene prompt followed by the shared style block into an image generator (Midjourney, Flux, Imagen, DALL·E).

## Shared style block

Photorealistic, ultra-high-definition luxury cosmetics photography, minimalist, premium and elegant. Palette of warm cream, dusty beige, light taupe and soft champagne gold, with a subtle hint of dusty rose. No pure white and no harsh contrast. Soft diffused natural daylight from a large window off to the left, even and warm, with gentle soft-edged shadows. Rich tactile textures. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, 4:5 vertical, 4K, editorial magazine quality. No text, logos or people.

## Negative prompt

pure white, harsh flash, hard shadows, neon, glitter, clutter, text, watermark, plastic look, oversaturated

## 1. Hero: plaster wall and travertine podium

A rounded travertine stone podium in beige against a warm cream lime-plaster wall with subtle hand-troweled texture. A single champagne-gold compact palette rests on top with the lid slightly open, and a soft shadow falls across the wall. Large empty negative space in the upper third for text overlay.

## 2. Velvet flat-lay

Top-down flat-lay on folded cream velvet with soft creases and a visible pile. A brushed champagne-gold makeup palette sits at the centre, with a gold-handled brush, a lipstick in dusty rose and a few loose powder swatches nearby. A dried pampas grass stem casts a faint shadow. Balanced, airy composition.

## 3. Window-light still life

A stack of two travertine slabs and a polished champagne-gold tray on a light taupe plaster surface. A row of cosmetic compacts and a perfume bottle in gold and cream stand on the tray. Window light creates a soft gradient across the wall and long, gentle shadows. Warm, quiet, luxurious.

## 4. Empty backdrop for product overlay

An empty premium background: a curved cream plaster wall meeting a beige travertine floor, with a slim brushed-gold rail and a folded velvet cloth in the corner. Soft window light, a subtle dusty-rose reflection, and a clear central area for placing product photos.
