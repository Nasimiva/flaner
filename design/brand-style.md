# flaner_cosmetics: visual style "Champagne Travertine"

Quiet-luxury look: warm stone backgrounds, gold hairlines, thin serif type, one hero product photo in an arch frame.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream Plaster | `#ECE0CC` | Main background (add soft plaster grain, light from the upper left) |
| Dusty Beige | `#D9C7AB` | Secondary surfaces, shadows on the background |
| Champagne Gold | `#B08D57` | Hairline frames, sub-headline, small labels, handle |
| Espresso Brown | `#4A3626` | Main headline, CTA text |
| Ivory | `#FAF4E8` | Logo circle fill, highlights |
| Dusty Rose (accent) | `#D9B3A5` | Rare accent only |

Never use pure white or pure black backgrounds.

## Typography

- Thin, high-contrast serif with Cyrillic support (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display Light, or Bodoni Moda).
- Headline: large, regular weight, Espresso Brown. Second line: italic, Champagne Gold, about half the size.
- Small labels (brand name, CTA): capitals with wide letter-spacing (about 0.25em), Champagne Gold or Espresso.

## Layout (9:16, 1080×1920)

1. Logo circle, top-left.
2. Brand label, then a two-line headline, centred, in the top third.
3. Product photo in an arch window with a 2px gold outline offset by about 26px and a soft warm shadow.
4. Short gold rule, CTA in small capitals, then `@flaner_cosmetics`.
5. Keep the key content inside the central 4:5 crop so the profile grid does not cut it.

## Photo rules

Warm colour grade, gentle contrast, no clutter, no app UI, no pure white. Gold packaging and black leather are the hero materials.
